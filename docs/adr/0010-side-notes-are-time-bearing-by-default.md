# Treat parenthesized side notes as time-bearing by default

Parenthesized gongche notes are parsed from `gc.raw` ranges as side notes and participate in rhythmic allocation, while staff notation displays them at a smaller size. They become zero-duration grace notes only when a reviewed qupai or qiangge profile says so, because visual smallness in Kunqu notation does not by itself mean the Western grace-note semantics.
