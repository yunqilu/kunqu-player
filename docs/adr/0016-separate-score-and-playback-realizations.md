# Keep one canonical score interpretation and a separate playback rendering

The converter will produce one canonical score interpretation from the reviewed relative score, and that interpretation is the sole source of note, rest, pitch, onset, and duration semantics in the one exported MusicXML document. A separate playback rendering consumes the canonical interpretation and may add versioned gongchepu.net-style pauses, timing adjustments, re-attacks, slides, vibrato, and synthesis expression to a Playback Plan, but it cannot own a second canonical onset or duration, export another MusicXML score, or mutate the score interpretation or relative score.

Playback-only effects remain visibly locatable in the canonical MusicXML through compact purple editorial markers without becoming notes, rests, or score durations. Each marker uses its concrete effect name without a redundant “试听” (shìtīng, "playback preview") prefix. Explicit qiangge names are green, canonical-score inferences are orange, and ordinary confirmed or derived staff notation is black; these scopes do not recolor one another. MusicXML displays staff notation rather than Gongche characters, while stable IDs preserve source lookup.
